Business Cards

Printed on one or both sides at the standard 3.5 x 2 inch size. The finish decides how the card looks and feels in the hand.

Finishes

  • MatteA smooth card with no shine. Text stays easy to read under any light, and you can write on it.
  • GlossyA shiny coating that makes colours and photos look brighter and richer.
  • Soft TouchA velvety coating with a smooth, suede-like feel in the hand.
  • Spot UVA clear, glossy coating on selected areas, such as your logo, so they shine against a matte card.
  • FoilMetallic gold or silver foil pressed onto your logo or name so it catches the light.
  • EmbossedYour logo or name raised out of the card so you can feel it under your thumb.

Options

  • Single or Double SidedPrint on the front only, or use the back for a map, a tagline or a second language.
  • Rounded CornersCorners trimmed to a soft curve instead of a sharp point.
  • Thick StockA heavier card that feels more substantial in the hand.

Spiral Binding

Reports, manuals and menus bound into a finished document, with a cover that protects it.

Binding

  • Spiral (Coil) BindingA plastic coil threaded through holes along the edge. The pages turn fully and lie flat.
  • Cerlox (Comb) BindingA plastic comb with teeth that clip through the pages. Pages can be added or removed later.
  • Wire-O BindingA metal double-wire binding with a neat, professional look.
  • Stapled BookletsPages folded and stapled along the spine, for programs, catalogues and short guides.

Covers and finishing

  • Clear Front CoverA clear plastic sheet on the front so the title page shows through.
  • Card Back CoverA stiff card on the back so the document holds its shape.
  • LaminatingA clear protective layer for menus, signs and certificates.
